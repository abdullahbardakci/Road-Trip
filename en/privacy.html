Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-01-01

Road Trip respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Account Data: For registered users, we collect name and email address.
  • Location Data: If you build routes from your current location or add favorites, your location may be stored in our database for those features.
  • App Data: Your saved places and routes (stored on your device and/or in cloud if sync is enabled).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics (e.g., screens viewed, feature usage) to improve the app.
  • Support Data: Information you provide when contacting us (e.g., email, device details you share).

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the app and services
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect and prevent abuse

Third-Party Services and Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use Google Maps API and Apple Maps interfaces for mapping and navigation. When using these, you are subject to Google’s and Apple’s terms and privacy policies. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the app, protected by agreements.

Your Choices

  • Delete your routes and places at any time from within the app.
  • You can permanently delete your account from Settings > Delete my account. This will remove all data associated with your account.
  • Contact us to request information about the data we collect.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions? Email us at roadtripdestek@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. We will update the effective date above when changes are made.
