Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-01-01

Road Trip respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • App Data: Your saved places and routes are stored on your device and/or in your iCloud (if enabled) to sync across devices.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous analytics (e.g., screens viewed, feature usage) to improve the app.
  • Support Data: If you contact us, we receive the information you provide (e.g., email, device details you share).

How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the app and services
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect and prevent abuse

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the app (e.g., analytics), bound by agreements to protect your data.

Your Choices

  • You can delete your routes and places at any time from within the app.
  • You can opt out of analytics where applicable in the app settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions? Email us at support@example.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. We will update the effective date above when changes are made.
