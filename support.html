Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I create a route? Search for places, tap the + button to add them, then save your route.
  • Can I open my route in Maps? Yes. Use the Open in Maps button to export to your preferred navigation app.
  • Does the app work offline? You can view saved routes offline. Some features like search require internet.
  • How do I report incorrect information? Send details to our support email and we’ll review quickly.
